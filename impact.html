Shared responsibility

Food safety starts with clear details.

Use these prompts to support a thoughtful pickup. This website cannot inspect food or guarantee it is safe.

◷

Preparation time

Share when food was cooked, packed or harvested. Keep the time specific and honest.

⌖

Pickup deadline

Set a realistic pickup window and agree the exact location directly with the receiver.

❄

Storage instructions

Explain whether food needs refrigeration, should stay covered, or should be reheated.

!

Allergen information

Name known allergens and possible cross-contact. Ask questions if anything is unclear.

Important: a listing does not guarantee food safety.

Information is supplied by the person who posts it. Donors and receivers should use their own judgment, verify freshness and ingredients, follow safe handling guidance, and decline food if they are unsure. FoodBridge PK is a classroom frontend demonstration, not a food inspection or delivery service.
