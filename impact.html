Before sharing or collecting

Food safety starts here.

Please check food carefully and follow local food-safety guidance.

◷

Preparation time

Share when food was prepared or packed so the receiver can make an informed choice.

❄

Safe storage

Keep food at a safe temperature and explain any storage or reheating instructions.

!

Allergens

List ingredients, known allergens and possible cross-contact. Ask if anything is unclear.

⌂

Collection

Confirm the center, pickup window and availability. Arrange your own transport.

A listing is not a food-safety guarantee.

FoodBridge cannot inspect food or guarantee its safety. Food businesses and receivers are responsible for checking freshness, handling and compliance with applicable local requirements. Do not share or accept food if you are unsure it is safe.
