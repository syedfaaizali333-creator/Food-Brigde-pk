Our Mission & Trust

About FoodBridge PK

Connecting food donors with local communities, NGOs, and welfare trusts to bridge the gap of food insecurity across Pakistan.

Our Vision & Organization

FoodBridge PK is a student-led community project designed to minimize food waste by enabling households, restaurants, and event organizers to share surplus food safely.

Through transparent trust verification, food hygiene guidelines, and community partnerships, we ensure that surplus food reaches deserving families with dignity.

Our Core Values

  • 🌱
    Sustainability: Reducing food wastage and environmental impact.
  • 🤝
    Trust & Transparency: Direct connection between donors and verified receivers.
  • 💚
    Community Welfare: Building a compassionate society where no food goes to waste.
