A more connected community

Food has a way of bringing us together.

FoodBridge PK is an initiative to connect surplus food with people and organizations who can put it to good use.

Our purpose

Less waste. More dignity.

We want good, surplus food from restaurants and food businesses to reach people instead of going to waste. FoodBridge connects verified businesses and welfare organizations around collection from FoodBridge centers.

Individuals and families can register once to find FoodBridge centers and partner meal locations, without needing to book a food listing.

How we work

Care at every step.

Restaurant and organization registrations are reviewed before they can use food-sharing tools. Clear details about preparation, allergens, storage, and collection help everyone make informed decisions.

FoodBridge does not provide delivery. Food businesses arrange for food to reach the selected center, and organizations arrange their own pickup. Please confirm availability before visiting.

Partners and locations

Locations are added by the FoodBridge team. We do not list an organization as a partner or publish an address until it has been confirmed.
